About

I build software and help people learn.

I build software from the first idea through launch and ongoing support. I like understanding the whole problem, working closely with people, and making sure the finished product is useful.

I work on websites, practical automation, and inclusive technology. I researched and built a wearable communication aid for Deaf students, and helped take a logistics service from idea to a working business.

I've also taught coding to children for years, including children with special educational needs. I want every child to have the chance to make things with technology, not just use it.
